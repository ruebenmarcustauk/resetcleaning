How extra time works

Our prices cover the typical time for a home of your size. When our cleaner arrives, they do a quick walkthrough. If your home needs more time than usual, we'll contact you before doing any extra work. You can approve extra time, billed in 30-minute blocks, or we'll focus on the essentials within your booked time. You'll never be charged for extra time you haven't approved.
