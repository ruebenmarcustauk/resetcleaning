Why Reset Cleaning

"We cannot decide whether your bond is returned — but we can make sure cleaning is never the reason it is not. That is what the guarantee is for."

Queensland property managers inspect against the REIQ exit condition report. A bond clean is not a regular clean with extra effort — it is a top-to-bottom reset measured against a specific standard, where oven racks, grout lines, and window tracks matter as much as the floors.

Every Reset Cleaning bond clean follows that checklist in full and comes with a 72-hour re-clean guarantee: if your property manager raises any cleaning-related issue at the exit inspection, we return and fix it at no charge.

The service

What's included

Our bond / end of lease clean includes everything in our deep clean, plus the extras property managers check at the exit inspection — Gold Coast wide. Every bond clean is cleaned to REIQ exit report standard.

Everything in a regular clean

  • Bathrooms — toilet, shower, basin, mirrors, vanity, floors
  • Kitchen — benchtops, sink, stovetop, outside of appliances
  • Splashback and cupboard fronts spot-cleaned
  • Bedrooms — beds made, dusting, tidy
  • Living areas — dusting, tables
  • All floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Bins emptied

  • Inside kitchen and bathroom cupboards and drawers (please empty them first)

  • Inside microwave
  • Full splashback
  • Grout scrubbed
  • Shower screen and taps descaled
  • Exhaust fan covers
  • Vacuum under and behind movable furniture

Plus bond clean extras
